DEWSBURY, England, Oct. 28, 2025 /PRNewswire/ — Jacquie Lawson, one of the largest international ecard brands, renowned for the quality of its art, animation and music, announces the launch of its 16th annual Digital Advent Calendar. Immersing users in a picturesque Christmas village, this year’s interactive calendar begins in a warm and cosy cottage nestled in a quiet landscape.

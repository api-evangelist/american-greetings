CLEVELAND, Nov. 5, 2025 /PRNewswire/ — In celebration of the 75th anniversary of Peanuts®, American Greetings is excited to announce the official launch of the all-new Peanuts Digital Advent Calendar 2025! Beginning today, fans can purchase the calendar and gain immediate access to select games and activities to enjoy ahead of the holiday season.
